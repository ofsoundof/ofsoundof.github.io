Research

My research focuses on AI efficiency: accelerating computation, reducing memory footprint, and mitigating power consumption in AI systems, across both cloud and edge environments. Efficiency is rarely won at a single layer; the largest gains come from evolving the algorithm and the hardware that runs it together.

Applications Vision Language Embodied AI Biosignals
Co-design
Efficient model designTransformers, state-space models, foundation models

What is the right computational mechanism for a given architecture? I have studied this for CNNs, graph neural networks, vision Transformers (LocalViT, GRL, SemanIR, VRT), and state-space models (MambaIR, FEMBA).

Model compression & deploymentQuantization, pruning, low-rank adaptation, token pruning

How much of a trained model is actually needed? My work spans weight pruning (group sparsity, random pruning, DHP), token pruning (FastVAR), quantization (SliM-LLM, OBR), tensor decomposition (learning filter basis), and low-rank adaptation (IntLoRA), applied to LLMs and diffusion models.

Software–hardware co-designDataflow, kernels, and scheduling from clusters to the edge

Once the model is fixed, the bottleneck moves to how it is mapped onto silicon. I work on serving modern models efficiently both on large clusters (FlatAttention) and on severely constrained edge devices (smart glasses, Retina, TinyTracker).

Hardware architecture & EDARISC-V processors, AI accelerators, PPA optimization, 3D integration

Open-source processor and AI accelerator architectures based on RISC-V, as well as EDA methodologies for hardware PPA (Power, Performance, Area) optimization and 3D integration. This is the newest direction in the lab, growing out of my work at ETH Zürich with Prof. Luca Benini's group.

Foundation models for biosignalsApplication focus · EEG, EMG, ECG, ultrasound

Biosignals record physiological activity and underpin healthcare, disease monitoring, BCI, HCI and AR/VR. They demand accurate modelling under a tight on-device compute budget — here efficiency is not an optimization but a precondition. This is my most active recent direction (PhysioWave, LUNA, WaveFormer, FEMBA, CEReBrO).
