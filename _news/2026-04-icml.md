Two papers are accepted to ICML 2026!

The first paper proposes <a href= "https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22709" class="text-blue" target="_blank"> GRACE</a>, a confidence-based distillation method for efficient VLMs.
The second paper proposes <a href= "https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/poster/62262" class="text-blue" target="_blank"> Brain Signal Rendering</a>, a encoder that renders EEG signals as a
Spectrum Video and learns robust, layout
agnostic spatial-temporal representations for better generalization across EEG datasets.
