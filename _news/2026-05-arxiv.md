Two papers are released on arxiv. [RDKV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08317v1) proposes a rate-distortion bit allocation method for KV cache compression.
In this paper, we differentiate the compression-tolerant components from the sensitive components and propose a joint token eviction
and quantization method.
In [Beyond GSD-as-Token](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07562), we propose a parameter-efficient fine-tuning framework remote sensing VLMs.
